# Receipt Reconciliation Example

## Scenario

Expected total:
R$ 1,415.57

Receipt total:
R$ 1,452.97

Difference:
R$ 37.40

## Investigation

The difference was caused by weighted products:

- Lemons
- Bananas
- Papaya
- Avocado

These products were intentionally excluded from the estimated total because their final value depended on checkout weight.

## Result

✅ Receipt successfully reconciled

✅ No pricing discrepancies found
