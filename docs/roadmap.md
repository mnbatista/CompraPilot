# 🗺️ CompraPilot Roadmap

This roadmap reflects the evolution of CompraPilot based on real shopping trips, receipt verification scenarios, and checkout validation use cases.

---

# ✅ Version 1.0 – MVP Foundation

Released

Core capabilities validated in real shopping scenarios.

## Delivered

- Shelf-label photo registration
- Quantity confirmation
- Promotional price interpretation
- Minimum-quantity promotion validation
- Real-time subtotal calculation
- Real-time shopping total calculation
- Item correction
- Item removal
- Receipt analysis
- Checkout total comparison
- Shopping list tracking
- Missing item identification

---

# ✅ Version 1.1 – Real-World Usability

Released

Focused on improving usability during actual shopping trips.

## Delivered

- Compact Shopping Mode
- Checkout Mode
- Faster item lookup
- Reduced scrolling during shopping
- Structured shopping summaries
- Improved receipt reconciliation
- Support for separate discounts on receipts

## Real-world drivers

- Long conversations made checkout verification difficult.
- Shelf-label evidence needed to be found faster.
- Mobile shopping required shorter responses.

## Validation targets

- Large grocery receipts
- Wholesale store receipts
- Mixed promotion scenarios
- Real-world discrepancy detection

# 🚧 Version 1.3 – Shopping Intelligence

Planned

Instead of simply identifying a promotion, CompraPilot should be able to explain:

- potential savings;

# Long-Term Vision

CompraPilot started as a simple question:

> "Is the price charged at checkout the same as the price shown on the shelf?"

The long-term goal is to evolve that simple idea into an AI-powered shopping copilot that helps consumers understand what they are buying, why they are paying that amount, and how they can make better shopping decisions.
